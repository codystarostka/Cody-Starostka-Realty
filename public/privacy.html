Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information Cody Starostka (eXp Realty, The Axon Group) collects through codystarostka.com and how it is used.

Information I collect

When you fill out a form on this site, I collect what you enter: your name, property address, phone number or email address, whether you are looking to sell, buy or both, and how you prefer to be contacted. If you subscribe to updates, I collect your email address.

How I use it

I use your information to respond to your request, prepare a home value report and pricing breakdown, and follow up about buying or selling a home. I may contact you by phone call, text message or email.

Text messages

By submitting a form you agree to be contacted by call, text or email about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text message to opt out.

Who sees it

Form submissions are processed by Formspree, a third-party form service, and delivered to me by email. I do not sell your personal information. I may share it with others involved in your transaction, such as my brokerage, lenders, title companies or other agents, only as needed to help with your request, or when required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This site stores a small note in your browser so a pop-up is not shown to you repeatedly during a visit. If analytics tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated.

Your choices
